# Lady Elaydren d'Cannith

**Type:** NPC — Patron / Ally  
**Affiliation:** [[House Cannith]] (Breland enclave)  
**Location:** [[Sharn]], [[Breland]]

## Overview

Lady Elaydren d'Cannith is a semi-independent House Cannith operative in Breland who serves as a recurring patron and information source. She carries the Mark of Making and pursues lost pre-Mourning Cannith creation schemas with quiet urgency.

## Details

Elaydren occupies a delicate position within the fractured post-Mourning House Cannith. With the house split into Cannith South, East, and West following the loss of their patriarch in the Mourning, she operates with semi-independent authority — beholden to no single faction leader, which both protects and endangers her.

She hires adventurers regularly for retrieval and investigation work, paying well and asking few questions about methods. Her goals center on recovering warforged creation schemas and pre-war Cannith research, which she believes is critical to House Cannith reasserting its dominance in the post-war economy.

She is pragmatic, professionally warm, and discreet. She does not waste time on sentimentality but treats reliable agents with genuine respect.

## Campaign Relevance

Elaydren has hired the party previously and remains a valuable contact. She has particular interest in a Creation Pattern rumored to be hidden in vaults beneath [[Sharn]], and her intelligence suggests the [[Vault of Whispers]] may contain Cannith research she considers proprietary. She has also engaged [[The Whisper]] as an information source, which may create complications.
