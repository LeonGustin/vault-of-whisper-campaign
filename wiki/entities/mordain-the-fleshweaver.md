# Mordain the Fleshweaver

**Type:** NPC — Villain / Neutral Contact  
**Affiliation:** Formerly [[House Phiarlan]] (excoriate); now independent  
**Location:** [[Blackroot]] tower, [[Droaam]]

## Overview

Mordain the Fleshweaver is one of Eberron's most dangerous arcane eccentrics — an excoriate of House Phiarlan who survived the Twelve's attempts to eliminate him and retreated to Droaam, where he now conducts forbidden flesh-warping experiments inspired by the Daelkyr of Xoriat.

## Details

Once among House Phiarlan's most gifted operatives, Mordain's obsession with Xoriat-derived transmutation magic led him to perform experiments the house deemed heretical. After excoriation (ritual stripping of house status), he was targeted for elimination. He survived and now dwells in a tower called Blackroot, deep in Droaam's interior, surrounded by living hybrid creatures of his own creation — his "children."

Mordain is neither reliably hostile nor reliably allied. He trades knowledge and services in exchange for things he cannot obtain in Droaam: rare specimens, arcane texts, or access to unusual planar phenomena. He respects capability and despises weakness.

He is suspected — though not confirmed — to bear an [[Aberrant Dragonmark]], which may account for both his unusual power and the Twelve's original desire to see him destroyed.

## Campaign Relevance

Mordain has detailed knowledge of Daelkyr-influenced sites across Khorvaire and may hold information about the [[Vault of Whispers]]. His relationship with [[The Daughters of Sora Kell]] gives him intelligence that flows through Droaam. Approaching him requires careful negotiation; he has no reason to help unless the party can offer something he values.
