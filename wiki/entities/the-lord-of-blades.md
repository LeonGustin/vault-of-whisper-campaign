# The Lord of Blades

**Type:** NPC — Villain / Warlord  
**Affiliation:** Independent Warforged Nation (self-proclaimed)  
**Location:** [[The Mournland]]

## Overview

The Lord of Blades is a warforged warlord of unknown identity who rules a militant faction deep within the Mournland. He claims the shattered remnants of Cyre as rightful warforged territory and is actively hostile to any House Cannith presence.

## Details

His true name and original designation are unknown — some believe he is among the first warforged ever built by House Cannith, while others claim the Mourning itself transformed a lesser construct into something more powerful. He wears ornate bladed plate armor and commands total loyalty from his forces.

The Lord of Blades interprets the Treaty of Thronehold not as liberation for warforged but as continued subjugation — treating living constructs as property to be "freed" rather than people to be recognized. His goal is to claim all former Cyran creation forges and produce warforged in numbers enough to be ungovernable.

He actively hunts agents of House Cannith within the Mournland. Any expedition into that region risks crossing his claimed territory.

## Campaign Relevance

Any Mournland expedition to reach the [[Vault of Whispers]] must account for Lord of Blades patrols. He may have already discovered — or be actively searching for — the Vault's location. His relationship with [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]] is adversarial; she represents everything he despises. Whether he is purely an obstacle, a potential ally against a greater threat, or both depends on how the party approaches him.
