# The Whisper

**Type:** NPC — Information Broker / Unknown Alignment
**Affiliation:** None confirmed
**Location:** [[Sharn]], [[Lower Dura]] (Callestan district)

## Overview

"The Whisper" is an information broker operating out of Sharn's underworld, almost certainly a changeling, who deals exclusively in high-value secrets. They appear to have a direct and personal interest in the [[Vault of Whispers]] — suggesting their operational alias may not be coincidental.

## Details

The Whisper never meets clients directly. Communications arrive via enchanted sending stones deposited at dead drops throughout Sharn. Intermediaries who have worked with The Whisper describe glamoured figures who speak little and observe much; whether these are the same individual is unknown.

Their network spans all Five Nations. Unlike most information brokers who sell intelligence to the highest bidder, The Whisper has one strict rule: they never sell the same secret to competing parties. This gives them a reputation for trustworthiness that is unusual in the trade — and makes them valuable to serious clients.

The Whisper's interest in the Vault of Whispers predates the party's involvement. Whether they are a rival, a fellow seeker, or something else entirely has not been established.

## Campaign Relevance

The Whisper is the campaign's central mystery figure. They have sold information to [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]], the [[Boromar Clan]], and an unknown Aundairian party. At least one piece of information they sold — location data for a Cannith facility in [[The Mournland]] — proved accurate. Their true identity, their relationship to the Vault's name, and their ultimate goal are open questions.
