# The Daughters of Sora Kell

**Type:** NPC Group — Rulers / Power Brokers  
**Affiliation:** Independent — rulers of [[Droaam]]  
**Location:** Graywall and the Great Crag, [[Droaam]]

## Overview

The Daughters of Sora Kell are three hag sisters who rule Droaam — the unrecognized monster nation west of Breland. Sora Kell (green hag), Sora Maenya (annis hag), and Sora Teraza (dusk hag) are among the most dangerous political actors in Khorvaire, and Sora Teraza's prophetic abilities make the trio uniquely valuable and dangerous to any faction.

## Details

**Sora Kell** is the political intellect — ancient even by hag standards, she has been manipulating Khorvaire's rulers and factions for centuries. Droaam's unlikely stability under monster rule is her achievement.

**Sora Maenya** is the enforcer. An annis hag of immense physical power, she keeps Droaam's fractious warlords in line through fear and force. Even giants hesitate before her.

**Sora Teraza** is the oracle. Her prophetic sight extends across timelines; she rarely gives direct answers, but she is never entirely wrong. Those who receive her visions often don't understand them until it is too late.

Droaam was not recognized by the Treaty of Thronehold, but the Daughters secured a de facto cease-fire from Breland by demonstrating their nation was too costly to conquer. They now profit from mercenary contracts through House Deneith and trade in monster-derived alchemical reagents.

## Campaign Relevance

[[Mordain the Fleshweaver]] has documented dealings with the Daughters, making them an indirect intelligence source about events in Droaam. More critically, Sora Teraza may already know something about the [[Vault of Whispers]] — whether the party learns this is another matter. Any approach to Mordain risks pulling the party into Droaam's political web.
