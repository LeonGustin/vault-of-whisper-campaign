# The Mournland

**Type:** Region — Hazardous / Contested
**Former Nation:** [[Cyre]]
**Manifest Zone:** [[Mabar]] (Endless Night) — suspected

## Overview

The Mournland is all that remains of the nation of Cyre, destroyed on the Day of Mourning (Olarune 20, 994 YK). A wall of dead-grey mist marks its borders. Within, wounds do not heal, bodies do not decay, and the laws of nature operate erratically. The cause of the Mourning remains officially unknown.

## Details

**Environmental Hazards**
- Wounds sustained inside the Mournland do not heal naturally or magically while within its borders
- The dead do not decay — battlefields are frozen at the moment of the Mourning
- The landscape itself was crystallized mid-destruction in many areas
- Occasional "Mourning storms" of wild magical energy sweep the interior

**Factions**
- **The Lord of Blades** commands a warforged nation from a fortress believed to be somewhere in the central Mournland. He preaches warforged supremacy and actively hunts for pre-war Cannith schematics.
- **Ghost Armies** — preserved units of Cyran and enemy soldiers, some still fighting, confused about the war's end
- **Cyran Survivors** — small communities of mutated survivors cling to the borders

**The Cause**
The most credible theories: a House Cannith superweapon test gone catastrophically wrong; a deliberate strike by one of the other Five Nations; a Daelkyr incursion; or something within Cyre itself. Every nation denies responsibility.

## Campaign Relevance

The [[Vault of Whispers]] is rumored to be located within the Mournland, possibly within former Cyran military infrastructure. [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]] has specific interest in Cannith facilities here. [[The Lord of Blades]] would be a hostile presence for any expedition. The Mournland's healing-suppression effect makes it extremely dangerous for extended operations.
