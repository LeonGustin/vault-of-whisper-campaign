# Sharn, City of Towers

**Type:** City — Major Metropolis
**Nation:** [[Breland]]
**Manifest Zone:** [[Syrania]] (Azure Sky)

## Overview

Sharn is the largest city in Khorvaire and the campaign's primary base of operations. It rises from the cliffs above the Dagger River in impossibly tall towers, empowered by a manifest zone tied to Syrania that makes levitation and flight magic significantly more potent throughout the city.

## Details

Sharn is organized into vertical tiers of districts stacked atop each other, each with its own character:

| Tier | Character | Notable |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Upper City | Wealthy, powerful, political | City Council, dragonmarked enclaves |
| Middle City | Commercial, working class | Adventurers, merchants, guilds |
| Lower City (Dura) | Poor, dangerous, criminal | Boromar Clan, The Whisper's territory |
| Cogs (below) | Industrial, warforged labor | Foundries, elemental binding |

The Lord Mayor ([[Cathan ir'Demell]]) nominally governs, but real power is distributed among the [[City Council]], the dragonmarked houses, and organized crime (primarily the [[Boromar Clan]]).

The [[Sharn Watch]] maintains order inconsistently. Corruption is endemic in the lower wards.

## Campaign Relevance

Sharn is the party's home base. [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]] operates from her enclave here. [[The Whisper]] is believed to use the Callestan district in Lower Dura as their base. The vaults beneath the city reportedly contain pre-war Cannith creation schemas of interest.
