# Vault of Whispers

**Type:** Location — Arcane Repository / Campaign-Central  
**Nation:** Unknown (former [[Cyre]] territory)  
**Current Status:** Lost; believed within [[The Mournland]]

## Overview

The Vault of Whispers is a pre-Galifaran arcane repository of unknown construction, believed to contain dozens of bound extraplanar entities imprisoned within individual "whisper chambers." Its location within the Mournland has never been confirmed, and its current state — intact, damaged, or breached — is unknown.

## Details

The Vault predates the founding of Galifar by at least three centuries. Fragmentary records in the Korranberg Chronicle and certain House Cannith deep archives reference a "Chamber of Bound Voices" constructed by pre-war artificers and binding specialists operating under an unnamed Cyran noble house. The builders appear to have been deliberately erased from the historical record.

According to the best available — and contradictory — sources, the Vault contains:
- Multiple entities imprisoned in isolated chambers, each sealed with a distinct binding mechanism
- A range of entity types: elementals, entities associated with the Daelkyr and Xoriat, and at least one described only as "the First Voice"
- A master control mechanism built into the Vault's architecture, intended to keep all seals permanent

The Day of Mourning may have damaged or altered the Vault's seals. If any bound entity was freed by the catastrophe, it would represent a threat of unknown magnitude.

## Campaign Relevance

The Vault is the campaign's central objective. [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]] wants something from it — possibly a Cannith creation schema hidden inside. [[The Whisper]]'s connection to the Vault is the campaign's deepest mystery. The [[The Lord of Blades|Lord of Blades]] may be racing to find it independently. Getting there requires surviving [[The Mournland]], and whatever condition the Vault is in will define the campaign's endgame.
