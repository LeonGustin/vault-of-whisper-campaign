# Cyre

**Type:** Location — Former Nation (destroyed 994 YK)  
**Nation:** Cyre (defunct)  
**Current Status:** [[The Mournland]]

## Overview

Cyre was the cultural heart of the Galifar empire — renowned across Khorvaire for artistry, craftsmanship, and arcane innovation. On Olarune 20, 994 YK, an unknown magical catastrophe called the Day of Mourning obliterated the nation entirely, leaving only the dead grey wasteland now known as the Mournland.

## Details

Pre-war Cyre was considered the most culturally refined of the Five Nations. Its capital, Metrol, housed some of Khorvaire's greatest museums, theaters, and magical academies. House Cannith maintained its largest manufacturing operations in Cyre, including the creation forges responsible for producing most warforged soldiers during the Last War.

The Day of Mourning struck without warning. A grey mist swept outward from somewhere in Cyre's interior, and when it settled, nothing living within the borders had survived. The cause remains one of Khorvaire's greatest unsolved mysteries — theories range from a weapon of mass destruction to a planar collapse to divine judgment.

Cyran survivors — soldiers, diplomats, and travelers who happened to be outside the borders at the time — form the Cyran diaspora, now scattered across the Five Nations. The settlement of New Cyre in Breland hosts the largest concentration but is not recognized as a sovereign state. Cyrans carry a collective grief and identity that make them a distinct political and cultural force.

## Campaign Relevance

The [[Vault of Whispers]] is believed to lie somewhere within former Cyre, now the Mournland. Understanding Cyre's pre-war geography — especially the location of House Cannith forgehold complexes — may be critical to locating the Vault. Surviving Cyran scholars in New Cyre or [[Wroat]] may hold maps or records that no longer exist in any other form.
