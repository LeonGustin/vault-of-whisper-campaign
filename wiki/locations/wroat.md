# Wroat

**Type:** City — National Capital  
**Nation:** [[Breland]]  
**Ruler:** [[King Boranel ir'Wynarn]] (Parliament co-governs)

## Overview

Wroat is the political capital of Breland, seat of the Brelish Parliament and home to the Citadel — Breland's intelligence and law enforcement agency. Where [[Sharn]] drives Breland's commerce, Wroat drives its politics.

## Details

Wroat sits at the confluence of the Howling and Lurching rivers in central Breland. It is more orderly and less vertically imposing than Sharn, its architecture reflecting stability and institutional power rather than commercial ambition.

**Notable Sites**
- **Brelish Parliament** — bicameral legislature with genuine power; the lower house (Galifar's Parliament) is elected by landowners and guild representatives
- **The Citadel** — national intelligence and law enforcement; operates throughout Breland with broad authority; increasingly autonomous under its current director
- **Boldrei's Hearth** — the most politically significant inn in Khorvaire; parliamentarians, foreign dignitaries, and spies have all done business over its tables
- **Shining Valley** — noble quarter; embassies from all Five Nations and beyond

**Political Climate (996 YK)**
King Boranel's advancing age has created a succession crisis — his children are capable but not commanding. A significant parliamentary faction advocates for a republic. The Citadel's independence is growing, and it may act beyond its mandate if the succession question destabilizes the crown.

## Campaign Relevance

Information obtained through [[The Whisper]] suggests an unknown Aundairian party made contact with a Citadel officer in Wroat — possibly to purchase intelligence about the Vault's location before the party became involved. The Citadel connection warrants investigation.
