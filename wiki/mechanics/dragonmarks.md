# Dragonmarks

**Type:** Mechanic — Hereditary Magic / Social System
**Scope:** World-spanning (all of Khorvaire)

## Overview

Dragonmarks are hereditary arcane sigils that manifest on members of specific bloodlines, granting spell-like abilities and forming the foundation of the dragonmarked house system — the economic guilds that dominate commerce across Khorvaire. There are twelve recognized marks (the Mark of Death was destroyed millennia ago), each associated with one or two noble families organized into a "house."

## The Twelve Houses

| Mark | House | Domain |
|------|-------|--------|
| Detection | House Medani | Divination, warning |
| Finding | House Tharashk | Locating people/objects |
| Handling | House Vadalis | Animal breeding/control |
| Healing | House Jorasco | Curative magic |
| Hospitality | House Ghallanda | Food, shelter, comfort |

| Storm | House Lyrandar | Wind, weather, elemental binding |
| Warding | House Kundarak | Banking, magical security |

## Mark Power Levels

- **Least Mark** — minor cantrip-level abilities; common in house bloodlines
- **Lesser Mark** — significant utility spells; respected within the house
- **Greater Mark** — powerful spell access; house leadership tier
- **Siberys Mark** — full-body manifestation; extraordinarily rare; near-mythic power

## Aberrant Dragonmarks

Marks appearing outside recognized bloodlines, or combining traits of multiple marks, are "aberrant." They are unpredictable, often grotesque, and historically persecuted by the Twelve. The Last War saw organized aberrant-marked individuals band together briefly. [[Mordain the Fleshweaver]] is suspected to bear an aberrant mark.

## Game Mechanics

Characters with dragonmarks gain access to the associated spells (specific to mark level). The house connection also provides social access and guild resources — at the cost of house obligations. Aberrant marks provide power without the social infrastructure, but carry significant stigma.
