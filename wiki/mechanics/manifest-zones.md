# Manifest Zones

**Type:** Mechanic — Planar Magic / Environmental
**Scope:** Specific geographic locations across Eberron

## Overview

Manifest zones are geographic areas where the boundary between Eberron and one of the thirteen Outer Planes is permanently thin. Unlike the cyclical coterminous/remote alignment of the planes, manifest zones maintain their planar connection regardless of the current alignment cycle, creating persistent local magical effects.

## How They Work

The thirteen planes orbit through cycles of being coterminous (close, strong influence) or remote (distant, weak influence) with Eberron. In a manifest zone, one plane's influence is always present — effectively, it is always at least partially coterminous at that location.

The strength and nature of effects depends on:
- Which plane the zone is tied to
- The size and intensity of the zone
- Whether a character or object is attuned to that plane

## Notable Manifest Zones

| Location | Plane | Primary Effect |
|----------|-------|----------------|
| [[Sharn]] | Syrania (Azure Sky) | Flight/levitation empowered; enables Sharn's tower heights |
| Dreadhold Prison | Dolurrh (Realm of the Dead) | Suppresses resurrection; used deliberately |
| [[The Mournland]] (contested) | Mabar (Endless Night) | Wounds don't heal; undead strengthened; light suppressed |
| Manifest, Aundair | Fernia (Sea of Fire) | Pyromantic amplification; extreme ambient heat |
| Various Fey Crossings | Thelanis (Faerie Court) | Enchantment amplified; fey incursions |

## Game Mechanics

Within a manifest zone:
- Spells aligned with the associated plane are heightened (+1 effective spell level)
- Opposed spells may be suppressed or require DC 15 concentration checks
- Environmental effects apply continuously (flight in Sharn, no healing in the Mournland)
- Portals to the associated plane may be opened more easily (DC reduced by 5)
- Some zones grant or suppress specific creature abilities

## Campaign Relevance

The [[Vault of Whispers]]' rumored location in [[The Mournland]] places it within a suspected Mabar manifest zone — meaning healing magic will be suppressed during any expedition. [[Sharn]]'s Syranian zone is a strategic asset for aerial operations and should be considered when planning escapes or pursuits in the city.
