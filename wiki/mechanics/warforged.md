# Warforged

**Type:** Mechanic — Race / Constructed Humanoid  
**Scope:** Khorvaire-wide; concentrated in the Mournland and Five Nations

## Overview

Warforged are sentient constructs created by House Cannith as soldiers for the Last War. The Treaty of Thronehold granted them legal personhood and freedom from servitude in 996 YK, but social integration has been slow and contested. Tens of thousands of warforged now navigate Khorvaire without a homeland, economic footing, or clear legal protections beyond the treaty text.

## How They Work

Warforged are built from a magical composite of wood, stone, and metal, animated by a bound creation spark. They do not need food, water, or sleep, though they can enter a rest state to recover. They feel pain and emotion, develop distinct personalities through experience, and can be permanently killed.

House Cannith's creation forges — the arcane factories that produced warforged — were concentrated in [[Cyre]]. The Day of Mourning destroyed nearly all of them. The few remaining forges are heavily guarded secrets, severely limiting the ability to create new warforged.

## Game Mechanics

Warforged player characters:
- Immune to disease, poison, sleep, and paralysis
- Do not need food, water, or air (but still benefit from rest)
- +1 AC from composite plating (cannot wear armor without modification)
- Living construct subtype: affected by both creature-targeting and object-targeting magic

## Campaign Relevance

The [[The Lord of Blades|Lord of Blades]]' warforged forces in [[The Mournland]] represent the most direct warforged threat to any expedition. The creation forges hidden somewhere in former [[Cyre]] — possibly near the [[Vault of Whispers]] — are a significant secondary objective for [[Lady Elaydren d'Cannith]] and a prize the Lord of Blades desperately wants.
